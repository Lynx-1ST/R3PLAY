# R3PLAYX 2.9.7-dev.4

Further resource optimizations, preserving playback and display quality:

- Share song metadata across reordered queues and playlist views without losing duplicate songs or playback order.
- Limit large metadata request bursts and cancel unused requests when navigating away.
- Park the ambient-volume visual loop after focus loss and resume it when returning to the app.

Includes the Last.fm and artwork optimizations from dev.3. Measurements and validation are recorded in [the performance report](../performance-2.9.7-dev.4.md).
