# R3PLAYX 2.9.7-dev.3

Resource optimization preview. Audio quality, audio effects and artwork rendering remain unchanged.

- Remove idle positioning work from the audio-effects menu.
- Stop Last.fm playback sampling while paused, disconnected or scrobbling-disabled; sample active playback every two seconds.
- Replace Last.fm status polling with native change notifications and focus refreshes.
- Cancel waiting artwork searches immediately when leaving a page.
- Persist matched artwork and no-match results across app restarts with a bounded cache.
- Cancel old metadata work and reset scheduling on account changes.

Validation and measured before/after results: [performance report](../performance-2.9.7.md).
